ARENA3

A bounded, Quake 3-flavoured arena roguelike running on ioquake3 with OpenArena's free data set. Clear each room's bots, then walk into one of two portals — each portal carries a permanent upgrade. Clear 5 rooms to win.

WASD move  ·  Mouse look  ·  Click shoot  ·  1-9 weapons  ·  Esc menu
