DUNE CHOPPA // ANCESTORS

turn-based island war · gunship battles · no engine, no assets · pure canvas
dune choppa: ancestors — a turn-based war for the island.
each round pick 1 of 3 ancestors (1-3) to gain favor (~10 turns each), then play their 3 actions.
click a tile to act: produce (seeds), recruit (then 1/2/3 = soldier/archer/rider), muster (draft every city), build (flag/gold), trade, move (click your army, then a neighbouring tile).
taking a tile with an enemy army or a castle boots you into a live gunship battle — win it and the tile is yours.
space / end turn skips an action  ·  take the enemy castle to win  ·  lose yours and it's over.
battle: W A S D fly  ·  mouse aim  ·  click/hold minigun  ·  B bomb  ·  R restart.
part of the moai-heads desert series · sibling of dunechopa
