DUNE CHOPPA // ANCESTORS

turn-based island war · gunship battles · no engine, no assets · pure canvas
watch the AI play both sides — drafts, every action type, and the battles. loops forever.
dune choppa: ancestors — a turn-based war for the island, modern-warfare skin.
each round draft 1 of 3 commanders (1-3), then play their 3 actions.
logistics → supply / fuel / intel  ·  construct depots, barracks, intel centers & motor pools  ·  deploy units  ·  requisition trade  ·  reinforce drafts infantry at every barracks  ·  fire mission airstrike / EMP / supply drop  ·  maneuver click your army, then a neighbouring tile.
units are pure rock-paper-scissors: INF > ART > ARM > HEL > DRN > INF. battles auto-resolve from the unit mix — you just watch the playback and the tile changes hands.
space / end turn skips an action  ·  take the enemy HQ to win  ·  lose yours and it's over  ·  hold 3 objectives for 2 rounds to win.
R restarts after the war ends.
